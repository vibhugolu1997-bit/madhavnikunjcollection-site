    About Madhav Nikunj Collection

    कला में भक्ति, हर श्रृंगार में प्रेम।

    Devotion in craft, love in every shringar.

    Madhav Nikunj Collection (माधव निकुंज कलेक्शन) is an Indian boutique offering Laddu Gopal poshak, coordinating pagdi and devotional shringar.

    In a home that keeps Laddu Gopal, He is not an idol on a shelf. He is the child of the house: woken in the morning, bathed, dressed, offered bhog and put to rest at night. Choosing what He wears each day is part of that seva.

    Madhav Nikunj Collection exists for that one part of the day. We make poshak with matching pagdi for Laddu Gopal in sizes 0 to 6, and size 7 and above is made to order.

    This verse is the reason we take small things seriously. A leaf, a flower, a little water: what reaches Him is the love with which it is offered. A poshak is no different. It is a few inches of cloth, lace, mirrors and pearls, and it becomes an offering only when it is given with bhav. Our work is to make something worthy of that bhav.

    So every photograph here shows the actual piece, the price is shown for the size you choose, and the size guide explains how to measure your Laddu Gopal before you order. You can pay online or choose cash on delivery.

    Whichever poshak you choose, we hope it adds a little joy to your daily seva. Jai Shri Krishna.
