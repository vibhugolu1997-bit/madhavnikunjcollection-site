Size guide

A poshak is chosen by the size of your Laddu Gopal. Share either measurement below with your enquiry and Madhav Nikunj Collection will confirm the fit.

How to measure

  • A. Height: place the idol on a flat surface and measure straight up from the base to the top of the head, without the mukut.
  • B. Base width: measure across the widest part of the base.
  • If you already know your Laddu Gopal's size number, share that as well.

Sizes at Madhav Nikunj Collection

  • Sizes 0 to 6: choose the size number on the product page (0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6).
  • Size 7 and above: made to order. Choose “Size 7 or above — customisation request” on the product page and share your Laddu Gopal's size number or height; Madhav Nikunj Collection confirms the details, price and time needed with you.

Measurements for each size number have not been published yet. Share the height and base width above if you are unsure of the size number.
