Frequently asked questions

How do I order?

Add designs to your enquiry bag, set the quantity and preferred size, then copy the enquiry message and send it to Madhav Nikunj Collection. Madhav Nikunj Collection confirms the details with you before any order is placed.

Can I pay on this website?

Designs that show a Pay Now button can be paid for online. The payment is handled by Razorpay; this website does not see or store card or bank details. Other designs are by enquiry.

Why do designs say “Price on enquiry”?

A price is shown only once Madhav Nikunj Collection has confirmed it for that design. Otherwise the price is shared when you enquire.

Is the pagdi included?

Each product page says whether a pagdi appears in the photographs. What is included in the set is confirmed with your enquiry.

Which size should I choose?

Designs are made in sizes 0 to 6. For size 7 or above, choose the customisation request option and share your Laddu Gopal's size number or height. See the size guide for how to measure; Madhav Nikunj Collection confirms the fit with your enquiry.

Are these designs in stock?

This website does not show live stock. Availability is confirmed when you enquire.

Where are my favourites and bag saved?

Only on this device, in your browser. They are not sent anywhere. See privacy.
